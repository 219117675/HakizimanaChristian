# Agile Team Charter

*The shared purpose, values, working agreements and boundaries my team agreed on.*

## Evidence

The evidence for this section is the FundiShop Agile Team Charter. It identifies the team members, their assigned responsibilities, the project's purpose, team values, communication practices, working agreements, Definition of Done, risks and team sign-off. The charter provides a shared reference for how the team planned to collaborate and develop the FundiShop service marketplace.

![FundiShop Agile Team Charter showing the project title and team member details](images/fundishop-charter.png)

*Caption: FundiShop Agile Team Charter, prepared in 2026. The document records the project purpose, team members, assigned roles, working agreements and sign-off section.*

## Reflection

Our FundiShop team consisted of Kabelo Moloko, Lisakhanya Mpahla, Christian Hakizimana and Muhammad Siddeeq Rabin. We divided responsibilities according to our assigned roles to support collaboration and ensure that the main areas of the project were covered. Kabelo was assigned the role of Scrum Master and Project Coordinator, Lisakhanya was the Product Owner, Christian was responsible for backend and database development, and Muhammad was responsible for frontend development and quality assurance. Our team's purpose was to develop FundiShop, a web-based service marketplace that connects homeowners with local professionals, known as Fundis. The platform is intended to help homeowners find service providers and manage bookings while allowing Fundis to manage their profiles and booking requests.

The working agreements that mattered most were communication, task allocation, decision-making and the Definition of Done. Communication was important because the frontend and backend components needed to work together for the application to function correctly. Sharing progress and raising problems early helped the team understand what had been completed and what still needed attention. Clearly assigned responsibilities also helped each member understand their contribution to the project. The Definition of Done was especially important because writing code did not automatically mean that a feature was complete. A feature needed to satisfy its requirements, work as intended, be tested and integrate with the relevant parts of the system. Decisions about requirements and priorities also needed to support the project's overall objectives and the needs of homeowners and Fundis.

The charter provided a common reference for the team's responsibilities and agreed ways of working. It established expectations around collaboration, accountability, quality and communication. However, a charter is only useful when team members apply it throughout the project. Coordination was particularly important when frontend components, backend APIs and database functionality had to work together. When challenges occurred, the team needed to communicate clearly, identify the cause of the problem, clarify responsibilities and work towards a solution. **[Replace this sentence with a real example of a challenge your team experienced, how the team responded, and whether the charter helped.]** Reflecting on this experience helped me understand that creating a charter is only the first step; team members must continue to follow and review the agreements as the project progresses.

Through this project, I learned that successful teamwork requires more than dividing tasks among individuals. It requires accountability, mutual respect, clear communication and a willingness to help other members when challenges arise. I also learned that individual contributions must fit together to produce a working system. The Agile Team Charter helped establish shared expectations and provided a foundation for organising the team's work. It also connected to later project artefacts, including user stories, the product backlog, the sprint backlog and the Agile logbook. These artefacts help translate the team's overall purpose into requirements, prioritised tasks, planned work and records of progress. Overall, the charter helped me understand the importance of having a shared direction, assigning responsibilities clearly, checking the quality of completed work and improving the team's approach throughout the development process.
